Fix dropped replies on worker-eviction reconnect

When the service worker had been evicted and the user sent a message,
post() reconnected with a bare chrome.runtime.connect() that had no
onMessage listener. The request was delivered but the streamed reply
had nowhere to land, so the panel appeared to hang until the next
disconnect/reconnect cycle. Reconnect through the full connect() path
(listeners, heartbeat, resume) via a connectRef, so a cold worker no
longer silently swallows the answer.
